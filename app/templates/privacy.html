Privacy notice (draft)

MVP draft — to be reviewed before public launch.

What we store

  • Your email and a salted bcrypt hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Receipt files you upload and the details extracted from them (vendor, date, amount, category, notes).

How it's protected

  • Files are stored in a separate folder per account and are only served to the signed-in owner.
  • We do not sell, share or show your data to anyone else. No ads, no trackers.
  • Session cookies are signed and HTTP-only.

Processing

By default receipts are read on our own server (local OCR). {% if settings.extractor == 'openai' and settings.openai_api_key %} This deployment uses an AI provider (OpenAI) to read receipt images; images are sent to that provider for extraction. {% else %}No receipt data is sent to third-party AI services.{% endif %}

Your control

  • Edit or delete any receipt at any time.
  • Export everything as CSV/PDF.
  • Delete all your data, or your whole account, from the Account page. Deletion is immediate and permanent (backups, if any, expire on their normal schedule).
