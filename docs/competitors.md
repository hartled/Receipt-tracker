# Competitor scan — Receipt Tracker (US freelancers)

Prices are list prices from vendor pages and Oct 2026 roundups (finpresso.com, getsumly.com, slipsheet.app, waveapps.com, sparkreceipt.com). They change often, so check them again before using them in marketing.

| Product | Entry price (USD) | Key features | Gaps for a US solo freelancer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Expensify** | Free: 25 SmartScans/mo. Collect: $5/member/mo | Strong SmartScan OCR, email forwarding, mileage, QBO/Xero sync on Collect | Built for team expense reports and approvals. Produces no Schedule C view and gives no deduction guidance |
| **Shoeboxed** | Starter: $9/mo or $97/yr (about 30–50 scans/mo). Pro: $29/mo | Human-verified data entry, mail-in "Magic Envelope", mileage, CSV and audit-ready reports | Low scan caps and overage fees. QBO/Xero/Gmail sync only on Pro. No bank matching. Feels dated |
| **Dext** | $25.21/mo billed annually ($302.50/yr), 250 docs, 5 users | High-accuracy OCR, capture via email/WhatsApp, supplier rules, pushes to the ledger | Priced for bookkeepers and accountants. Line items cost extra. Too much for one person |
| Wave | Receipts-only: $8/mo. Pro: $19/mo | Unlimited OCR, email forwarding, full bookkeeping | The free tier has no receipt capture. You have to adopt Wave's whole ledger |
| Zoho Expense | Free: up to 3 users, 20 scans/user/mo. Standard: $3–4/user/mo | OCR, GPS mileage, 5 GB storage | Business and approval UI. No tax categories |
| QuickBooks Solopreneur | Free (2 receipts/mo). Lite: $20/mo | Receipts, mileage, TurboTax tie-in | QuickBooks Self-Employed is no longer sold to new customers. Lite costs a lot for just receipts |
| Everlance / Keeper | $10.99/mo; Keeper $20/mo | Mileage first (Everlance); AI deduction finder from bank feeds (Keeper) | Receipts are a side feature. Keeper needs bank access |

## Takeaways
1. **The price band is clear:** free tiers cap at 20–25 scans/mo, and simple paid plans run $5–9/mo. Above $19/mo you're paying for bookkeeping, not receipts.
2. **Nobody under $10 maps receipts to Schedule C lines for freelancers.** Most of these tools organize expense reports or feed a ledger. That's the gap we target.
3. **Email forwarding and good OCR are now expected.** Shoeboxed charges per scan and gates sync behind Pro, which users dislike.
4. **Privacy and simplicity are a real angle.** We need no bank connection, can delete all data in one click, and give a clean year-end CSV/PDF for the accountant or TurboTax.
5. **Risk:** OCR accuracy is where Expensify and Dext compete hardest. We should make manual correction fast rather than promise perfect extraction.
